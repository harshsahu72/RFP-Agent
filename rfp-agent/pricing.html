FAQ

Common questions

Can I import my existing proposals? +

Yes! You can upload PDF, Word, Google Docs, or plain text files individually or in bulk via ZIP. We also support direct integration with Google Drive, SharePoint, and Dropbox.

Is my proposal data secure? +

Absolutely. We are SOC 2 Type II certified. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We never use your data to train base models — your knowledge stays exclusively yours.

How long does setup take? +

Most teams are fully onboarded in under 30 minutes. Upload your first batch of proposals, connect your CRM (optional), and you're ready to generate your first AI draft. Enterprise customers get a dedicated onboarding specialist.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime and retain access until the end of your billing period. We'll export all your data in a portable format before you go.

Do you offer a free trial? +

Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to all features of your chosen plan. No credit card required to start.
