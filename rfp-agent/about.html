Our Story

Making proposal writing smarter

We believe no business should lose a deal they deserved to win because of a slow or mediocre proposal process.

Our Mission

Make proposal writing faster and smarter

RFP Agent was founded in 2022 by a team of former consultants and AI engineers who spent years drowning in spreadsheets, shared drives, and late-night proposal marathons. We knew there had to be a better way.

We built the tool we always wished we had — one that remembers everything, learns from outcomes, and helps you put your best work forward every time without starting from scratch.

Today we serve over 500 companies across 20+ industries, helping their teams write better proposals and win more of the deals they deserve.
